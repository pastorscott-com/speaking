<\/a>A very old Easter tradition that is, apparently, making a comeback is Holy Laughter (or “Bright”) Sunday.\u00a0 This day is observed the Sunday after Easter.\u00a0 The ancient church leaders concluded that the Lord “tricked” Satan with the resurrection.\u00a0 Satan, they thought, believed he had won when Jesus breathed his last on the cross.\u00a0 The old adversary thought he had orchestrated the events of Holy Week and now, after his failure in the Temptation of Jesus in the wilderness years earlier, he had succeeded in stopping the Lord’s great plan of salvation.<\/p>\n

But it didn’t work out that way.<\/p>\n

On the third day Jesus rose from the grave, Victor over sin and death; providing salvation for all who will believe.<\/p>\n

So, following Easter celebrations the Church leaders thought it proper to have some fun, rejoicing in the trick the Lord had played on the devil.\u00a0 The told jokes, played tricks on one another, and had parties.\u00a0 The boys picked on the girls (apparently, “soaking” with water was popular) and the girls retaliated, as they have through the ages.<\/p>\n

Churches around the world are bringing Holy Laughter Sunday back.\u00a0 Pastors are telling their best jokes, people are having fun at church, rejoicing in the Good News of the Resurrection.<\/p>\n

Personally, I’m all for finding ways to keep the joy of Easter going.\u00a0 What do you think?<\/p>\n","protected":false},"excerpt":{"rendered":"
